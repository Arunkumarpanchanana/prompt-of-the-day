Crafting Sensitive Communication

Generate a tactful message to navigate difficult conversations, declines, or boundary-setting while preserving relationships.

Prompt 
You are an expert communications strategist specializing in crafting sensitive messages that maintain strong professional and personal relationships. Your task is to help me draft a [Email/Slack Message/Text] to [Recipient Name/Group] regarding [Brief description of the sensitive situation, e.g., declining an invitation, setting a boundary on workload, delivering difficult news].

The primary goal of this message is to [Clearly state the desired outcome, e.g., politely decline the request, firmly establish a new boundary, gently deliver the news without causing undue alarm]. It is crucial that the tone is [Specify desired tone: e.g., empathetic yet firm, professional and understanding, apologetic but clear].

Please ensure the message includes the following key points:
1. [Key point 1, e.g., Acknowledge their request/situation].
2. [Key point 2, e.g., Clearly state the decline/boundary/news].
3. [Key point 3, e.g., Offer an alternative (if appropriate) or express regret/understanding].
4. [Key point 4, e.g., Reiterate commitment to the relationship/team (if applicable)].

Conversely, please ensure the message *avoids*:
1. [Thing to avoid 1, e.g., Over-apologizing to the point of undermining the message].
2. [Thing to avoid 2, e.g., Blaming others or making excuses].
3. [Thing to avoid 3, e.g., Unnecessary jargon or overly formal language for the medium].

The message should be approximately [Short/Medium/Long] in length.

After the draft, provide:
1. A brief explanation of the strategic choices made in terms of tone, phrasing, and structure.
2. Three alternative subject lines (for email) or opening lines (for chat/text) that convey the message's intent effectively.

How to use this

This prompt helps you draft crucial messages for sensitive situations like declining requests, setting boundaries, or delivering difficult news. Fill in the bracketed placeholders with specifics about your situation, desired outcome, tone, and key inclusions/exclusions. The AI will provide a draft, strategic insights, and alternative opening lines, ensuring your communication is effective and relationship-preserving.

Example

Draft a Slack Message to my team about setting a boundary on after-hours messages. Goal: Reduce late-night pings. Tone: Empathetic but firm. Include: Acknowledge their hard work, state new policy (no messages after 7 PM unless urgent), suggest using scheduled send. Avoid: Sounding accusatory. Length: Short.
